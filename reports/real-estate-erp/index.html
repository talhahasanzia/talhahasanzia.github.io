🗺️ Land & Patwar

Land Acquisition, Procurement & Revenue (Patwar) Record Management

Manages raw land acquisition from landowners and investors, traditional revenue/Patwar land hierarchies, inheritance/mutation tracking, land payment schedules, and litigation records.

Revenue / Patwar Land Record Hierarchy (Mouza, Khasra, Khewat, Khatoni)

6 capabilities
  • Mouza (Revenue Estate / Village) Master: Define and manage revenue estates and villages where housing society or project raw land is located, tracking total acreage, boundaries, and acquisition targets per Mouza.
  • Khewat (Ownership Account) Tracking: Record and manage landowner family/group ownership accounts (Khewat numbers), total shared area, and individual share ratios within each Mouza.
  • Khatoni (Cultivator / Occupancy Holding) Tracking: Maintain detailed occupancy and possession records (Khatoni numbers) linked to each Khewat, identifying actual physical possession holders and tenancy rights.
  • Khasra (Survey / Plot Number) Inventory: Maintain granular survey plot records (Khasra numbers) with exact measurement units (Kanal, Marla, Sarsai, Karam, Sq.Ft, Acre), soil/land classification, boundary coordinates, and acquisition status.
  • Warasat (Inheritance & Mutation / Intiqal) Management: Record legal inheritance mutations (Intiqal-e-Warasat), succession trees, legal heirs' fractional shares, and mutation approval statuses across revenue records.
  • Land Partners & Joint Venture (JV) Shares: Manage co-owners, joint landholders, and investor equity/land-sharing ratios in raw land parcels.

Land Procurement, Landowner Contracts & Seller Ledgers

8 capabilities
  • Landowner (Seller / Honor) Profile Creation: Capture complete landowner KYC including Name, Father/Husband Name, CNIC, Biometrics, Photographs, Contact Details, Bank Account Info, and legal representative/attorney details.
  • Land Acquisition Contracts & Sales Agreements: Draft and store raw land purchase agreements (Bayana / Agreement to Sell / Sale Deed), specifying Khasra numbers, rate per Acre/Kanal/Marla, total contract value, token money, and registry deadlines.
  • Land Buying via Standard Purchase Invoices (PI): Process land acquisition directly through the ERP procurement and accounting engine to capitalize raw land inventory on the balance sheet.
  • Land Payment Installment Plans & Barter/Plot Adjustments: Configure multi-stage cash payout schedules or land-for-developed-plots barter commitments for landowners.
  • Land Payments with Proof Attachments & Seller Ledgers: Record cash, cheque, pay-order, and bank transfer payments to landowners with scanned payment proofs, acknowledgment receipts, and real-time Seller Account Ledgers.
  • Land Title & Transfers History: Maintain an auditable chronological chain of title transfers, registries, and mutations from original landowners to the society/company.
  • Third-Party / Broker Commission on Land Procurement: Calculate, approve, and disburse land consolidator/broker commissions linked to specific Khasra/Mouza acquisitions.
  • Land Litigation, Stay Orders & Expense Tracking: Track court cases, stay orders, boundary disputes, legal counsel fees, stamp duty, and registry expenses against specific land parcels.
📐 Town Planning & GIS

Project Setup, Town Planning, Inventory & Interactive GIS / Digital Mapping

Manages multi-project hierarchies (housing societies, phases, blocks, streets, high-rise towers, and floors), plot/unit master inventory, and interactive web/mobile GIS maps for visual plot selection and booking.

Multi-Project, Phase, Block, Street & High-Rise Tower Hierarchy

4 capabilities
  • Multi-Project Enterprise Capacity: Manage 100+ concurrent real estate projects, housing schemes, commercial plazas, and apartment towers with both isolated project books and consolidated executive dashboards.
  • Town Planning Hierarchy (Horizontal Developments): Configure Society $\rightarrow$ Sub-Project $\rightarrow$ Phase $\rightarrow$ Sector $\rightarrow$ Block (e.g., Block A, Block A Commercial, Overseas Block, Executive Block, General Block) $\rightarrow$ Street / Lane / Road Width (30' Wide Road, 50' Wide Road, Main Boulevard) $\rightarrow$ Plot.
  • Vertical High-Rise Hierarchy (Malls, Towers & Apartments): Configure Project $\rightarrow$ Tower / Building $\rightarrow$ Floor (Basement, Ground, Mezzanine, 1st–Nth Floor, Penthouse) $\rightarrow$ Unit (Shop, Kiosk, Food Court/Canteen, Office, Studio/1/2/3/4-Bed Apartment, Parking Slot) with floor-by-floor summary ribbons and visual status grids.
  • Multi-Level Location Categorization: Support up to 7 hierarchical levels of spatial and administrative location coding across properties and fixed assets.

Property, Plot & Unit Inventory Master

6 capabilities
  • Multi-Category Property Support: Manage Residential Plots, Commercial Plots, Open/General Files, Constructed Houses, Villas, Farmhouses, Apartments/Flats, Retail Shops, Corporate Offices, Warehouses, and Amenity Sites (Parks, Mosques, Schools, Graveyards, Commercial Zones).
  • Granular Unit Attributes: Store unique Property ID, Plot/Unit Number, Street/Floor, Dimensions (Front $\times$ Depth), Total Area (Marla, Kanal, Sq.Yd, Sq.Ft, Sq.M), Base Rate per Sq.Ft / Marla, and Lump Sum Gross Value.
  • Location Preferences & Prime / Category Extra Charges: Define percentage or fixed prime location surcharges for Corner Plots, Park Facing, Main Boulevard, Three-Side Open, West Open, or Lobby/Atrium Facing units.
  • Pricing History & Plan Linkage: Track historical price revisions over time and link eligible installment plan templates to each unit category.
  • Real-Time Inventory Status Lifecycle: Track and color-code live unit states: Available / Vacant, On Hold, Reserved (Token Paid), Booked, Allotted, Possession Ready, Handed Over / Sold, Under Construction, Rented / Leased, Transferred, Cancelled, Terminated, Merged, Blocked / Under Litigation.
  • Property Media & Legal Attachments: Attach site photographs, 2D/3D floor plans, architectural layouts, and title documents to each inventory record.

Interactive Digital Society Map, GIS & Online Plot Booking (OMP)

6 capabilities
  • Master Map Cutting as per Block: Slice master AutoCAD/GIS town-planning layouts into interactive block-level digital maps for fast browser and mobile rendering.
  • Spatial Plot X & Y Coordinate Mapping: Assign exact polygon/point X & Y coordinates to every plot on the visual map canvas so each plot is clickable and interactive.
  • Google Maps Geo-Location & Multi-Level Drilldown: Overlay society master plans onto real-world Google Maps coordinates with interactive drilldown from Society Overview $\rightarrow$ Phase $\rightarrow$ Block $\rightarrow$ Street $\rightarrow$ Individual Plot.
  • Live Color-Coded Availability Overlay: Display real-time plot statuses (Available, Hold, Booked, Sold, Reserved) directly on the map across Desktop, Tablet/iPad, and Android/iOS devices.
  • Multi-Parameter Map Search & Highlighting: Instantly filter and highlight plots on the visual map by Plot/Property No, Type (Residential/Commercial), Block, Size (5 Marla, 10 Marla, 1 Kanal, etc.), Category (Corner, Park Facing), and Status.
  • Real-Time Digital Hold, Book & Un-Mark from Map: Authorized sales staff, marketing agencies, and registered dealers can click an available plot on the map to place a temporary Hold or initiate a Booking (instantly locking the plot across all users to prevent double-booking) or Un-Mark to release an expired hold back to available inventory.

Dynamic Installment Plan & Construction-Stage Schedule Engine

11 capabilities
  • Flexible Payment Schedule Generator: Build custom schedules or apply standard templates supporting Token, Booking / Down Payment (% or amount), Confirmation Charges, Allocation Charges, Digging / Development Charges, Periodic Installments (Monthly, Bi-Monthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly/Bi-Annual, Annual), Balloon Payments, and Possession % Payment.
  • Construction-Linked Milestone Billing (ON DUE / FUTURE PLAN):
  • Define construction-linked payment stages (e.g., Start of Work, Excavation/Digging, Plinth/Foundation, Ground Slab, Nth Floor Slab, Brick/Block Work, Plaster, Grey Structure Completion, Finishing) at the end of the client's schedule.
  • Milestone amounts remain unbilled (On Due / Future Plan) until the engineering team marks that construction stage complete on site, automatically making the milestone amount fall due on that date.
  • Single-Schedule Auto-Synchronized Client Ledger:
  • Maintain one unified schedule per booking where scheduled dues, partial or full receipts, adjustments/waivers, cumulative receivables, and running balances synchronize automatically without manual ledger postings.
  • Oldest-Due-First Auto-Allocation: Incoming payments automatically settle the oldest unpaid installment or partial balance first (with option for manual installment selection).
  • Mid-Schedule CURRENT ACCOUNT POSITION Line: Automatically computes total amount due up to the current month, total received to date, net current arrears or advance paid, and overall % Paid.
  • Plan Revisions, Rescheduling & Sub-Agreements:
  • Modify or reschedule active payment plans with supervisor approval and full audit logging.
  • Create Sub-Agreements (splitting a major installment or development charge into its own secondary sub-installment schedule with an independent receipt history).

  • Issue PDC Receipt Acknowledgements when customers submit post-dated cheque books/bundles upfront.

  • Automatically post Post-Dated Bank Receipt Vouchers (PBRV) to the client ledger upon cheque clearance or reverse/penalize upon dishonor.
  • Multi-Installment & Multi-Property Single Receipt: Allocate a single lump-sum payment receipt across multiple installments or across multiple plots/shops/units owned by the same client.
  • Controlled Receipt Editing, Reversal, Re-Adjustment & Voiding:
