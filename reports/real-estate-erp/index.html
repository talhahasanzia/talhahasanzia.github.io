🗺️ Land & Patwar

Land Acquisition, Procurement & Revenue (Patwar) Record Management

Manages raw land acquisition from landowners and investors, traditional revenue/Patwar land hierarchies, inheritance/mutation tracking, land payment schedules, and litigation records.

Revenue / Patwar Land Record Hierarchy (Mouza, Khasra, Khewat, Khatoni)

6 capabilities
  • Mouza (Revenue Estate / Village) Master: Define and manage revenue estates and villages where housing society or project raw land is located, tracking total acreage, boundaries, and acquisition targets per Mouza.
  • Khewat (Ownership Account) Tracking: Record and manage landowner family/group ownership accounts (Khewat numbers), total shared area, and individual share ratios within each Mouza.
  • Khatoni (Cultivator / Occupancy Holding) Tracking: Maintain detailed occupancy and possession records (Khatoni numbers) linked to each Khewat, identifying actual physical possession holders and tenancy rights.
  • Khasra (Survey / Plot Number) Inventory: Maintain granular survey plot records (Khasra numbers) with exact measurement units (Kanal, Marla, Sarsai, Karam, Sq.Ft, Acre), soil/land classification, boundary coordinates, and acquisition status.
  • Warasat (Inheritance & Mutation / Intiqal) Management: Record legal inheritance mutations (Intiqal-e-Warasat), succession trees, legal heirs' fractional shares, and mutation approval statuses across revenue records.
  • Land Partners & Joint Venture (JV) Shares: Manage co-owners, joint landholders, and investor equity/land-sharing ratios in raw land parcels.

Land Procurement, Landowner Contracts & Seller Ledgers

8 capabilities
  • Landowner (Seller / Honor) Profile Creation: Capture complete landowner KYC including Name, Father/Husband Name, CNIC, Biometrics, Photographs, Contact Details, Bank Account Info, and legal representative/attorney details.
  • Land Acquisition Contracts & Sales Agreements: Draft and store raw land purchase agreements (Bayana / Agreement to Sell / Sale Deed), specifying Khasra numbers, rate per Acre/Kanal/Marla, total contract value, token money, and registry deadlines.
  • Land Buying via Standard Purchase Invoices (PI): Process land acquisition directly through the ERP procurement and accounting engine to capitalize raw land inventory on the balance sheet.
  • Land Payment Installment Plans & Barter/Plot Adjustments: Configure multi-stage cash payout schedules or land-for-developed-plots barter commitments for landowners.
  • Land Payments with Proof Attachments & Seller Ledgers: Record cash, cheque, pay-order, and bank transfer payments to landowners with scanned payment proofs, acknowledgment receipts, and real-time Seller Account Ledgers.
  • Land Title & Transfers History: Maintain an auditable chronological chain of title transfers, registries, and mutations from original landowners to the society/company.
  • Third-Party / Broker Commission on Land Procurement: Calculate, approve, and disburse land consolidator/broker commissions linked to specific Khasra/Mouza acquisitions.
  • Land Litigation, Stay Orders & Expense Tracking: Track court cases, stay orders, boundary disputes, legal counsel fees, stamp duty, and registry expenses against specific land parcels.
